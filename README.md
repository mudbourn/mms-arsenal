# MMS Arsenal

Custom weapons for MMSLive01: the Armory of Destiny melee weapons and a port of
the Just Enough Guns firearms, ammo and throwables.

Licensed under GPL-3.0. The firearms are ported from Just Enough Guns by Rui Mao,
MigaMi and Leander (GPL-3.0); its sounds are CC0.

The odachi is based on "Odachi, Japanese sword" by barbodoji under CC BY 4.0; see
[docs/ATTRIBUTION.md](docs/ATTRIBUTION.md).

High-res models are converted to Minecraft-style geo models with
`tools/mc_convert`; see [docs/MODEL_CONVERTER.md](docs/MODEL_CONVERTER.md).
