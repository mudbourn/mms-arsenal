# Relics

Relics are rare trinkets found as world loot. Each one is themed after a
fashion armour kit and gives that kit's wearer a reason to wear it that costs
no Colt: the kit stays a cosmetic, and the relic carries the stats.

## Rules

- Loot only. No recipe, no trade, no shop listing, no killstreak crate.
- Worn, not held. Stats and procs apply only while the relic sits in a
  Trinkets slot.
- Fashion kits only. Kits that keep a vanilla look (Aerial Hell, Expanded
  Weaponry, Frostiful, Enderscape, the vanilla sets) are progression gear and
  get no relic.
- One relic per kit. Colour variants of one kit share a relic (the O-Yoroi
  colours, the two Iron Plates).
- Every relic sits in the same power band: one or two small stat lines and one
  proc on a cooldown. A relic should never beat progression armour; it should
  feel like a perk.
- Fantasy Armor kits already grant potion effects for a full set. Their relic
  turns that effect into a proc, so the relic carries the theme without
  handing out a permanent Strength or Regeneration.

## Power band

| Tier | Loot tier | Stat budget | Proc cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lesser | 1-2 | one line | 30-45 s |
| Greater | 3-4 | two lines | 20-30 s |

Stat lines are small: +2 max health, +1 armour, +0.5 armour toughness, +5%
movement speed, +8% attack speed, +6% attack damage, +10% knockback
resistance, +1 luck, +10% spell power, +6% ranged damage.

Procs use Spell Engine passive triggers. Effects run 5-10 s at level I unless
noted.

## Implementation

- Each relic is an item in `relic/RelicItems` with a `spell/<relic>.json`
  passive under `data/mms_arsenal/spell/`. Stats apply through Trinkets only
  while the relic is worn; they are not in the item's attribute component.
- A relic cannot be taken off while its proc is on cooldown, except in
  creative.
- Greater relics (two stat lines) sit in loot tiers 3-4, Lesser in tiers 1-2.
  The tier files are `data/rpg_series/tags/item/loot_tier/tier_N_relics.json`.
- Knockback resistance and swim speed are flat additions; the other percent
  stats multiply the wearer's base value.
- Spell Engine has no kill trigger. A "hit on a target below 20% health" proc
  fires on the hit that leaves the target low, not on the kill itself.
- Fall damage procs fire after the damage is dealt. Fall, fire, drowning and
  undead conditions use Spell Engine damage and entity type tags.
- Biome and time-of-day conditions do not exist, so those procs fire on any
  damage. "Evade" procs fire on a Combat Roll.
- Icons under `assets/mms_arsenal/textures/item/relic/` come from Kyrise's
  Free 16x16 RPG Icon Pack (CC BY 4.0); `ATTRIBUTION.md` maps each relic to
  its source icon.
